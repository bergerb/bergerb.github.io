**Key Takeaways:**
- The "free buffet" of AI compute is ending, making cost-per-token a major business factor.
- Strategic Project Management is now essential to control AI expenditures.
- Local AI deployment via NPUs and open-source models is a path for privacy and cost-efficiency.

The AI landscape is evolving rapidly, bringing both exciting opportunities and challenges. The free buffet of compute is over as AI providers turn off subsidies and move to **per-use** models. I think there are some very real disadvantages to this for the consumer of these products.  As of June 2026, this has been the first month and weeks since the change has occurred. The biggest disadvantage is not knowing how much something will cost before you run it. Paying a monthly charge—this *known unknown*—was something you could work with; now rolling the dice is much riskier and more costly! For example, a complex coding task that used to be covered by a flat monthly fee might now cost $0.50–$2.00 per run depending on the model's token complexity. 

> The more things change -- the more they stay the same. - Jean-Bapiste Alphonse Karr

With all these changes, there is a reality that **Project Management** is still king. To control AI spend, we must control token spend, and to control token spend, we need to be deliberate and focused on *what* we want to accomplish. Organizations that are planned 12 months, 18 months out with work that is scoped and curated will be able to leverage AI in a more efficient manner. Leveraging IT, business, and user experience to refine and build work items that are targeted to direct areas and not drift throughout the entire system will deliver the most value. AI will also play a role in building work that is more token efficient by being more targeted.

Understanding these management principles is crucial because the current landscape has shifted significantly:

## Current Landscape
- Smaller monthly *courtesy* AI allowance and higher per-use charges.
- Frontier LLM models are very powerful but **expensive**.
- Expansion of lightweight open-source LLMs (GEMMA4, DeepSeek-R1, Qwen-Coder).
- Growing importance of code-oriented models for developers.

## Local AI Deployment
I believe an area we could see a large shift to is local AI deployment to reduce costs and dependency on cloud services. I could really see developers running personal AI compute boxes—i.e., "companion cubes" (similar to high-powered Home Lab servers)—that host AI CPU/GPU combos. In reality, modern hardware like *Intel Core Ultra* and *AMD Ryzen AI* have dedicated Neural Processing Units (NPUs) designed for high-efficiency inference with lower power draw than traditional GPUs. Running local LLMs is easier than it has ever been.

### Advantages:
1. **Cost Efficiency**: Avoid expensive cloud compute.
2. **Privacy**: Keep data local and secure.
3. **Customization**: Tailor models to specific use cases.

### Challenges Faced:
- **Hardware Requirements**: Running large models locally needs significant computational resources.
- **Model Selection**: Choosing the right model for specific tasks can be complex.
- **Ecosystem Maturity**: Local AI tools and workflows are still maturing.

## Future 
There are a number of smaller models that I have been enjoying using:

### Open Source Innovation:

| Model | Best For | Key Strength |
|:---|:---|:---|
| **GEMMA4** | Local Deployment | High performance/efficiency |
| **DeepSeek-R1** | Reasoning/Logic | Strong open-source reasoning |
| **Qwen-Coder** | Development | Optimized for code completion |
| **Codestral** | Development | State-of-the-art code generation |

### Technical Advancements:
- Improved tools for local model management.
- Better support for running models on different operating systems.
- More user-friendly interfaces for AI development.

## Conclusion
The future of AI is bright but requires careful navigation. By leveraging open-source tools and focusing on local deployment, developers can unlock new possibilities while maintaining control and reducing costs.
